Stepping to the Street
You reach the busier part of the path, with less trees and more road. Cars fly by endlessly, keeping you from being able to cross. Motorcycles roar by, almost a blur. Heavy trucks squeak and bump along slowly. Speeding sports cars rev and zoom past. Here and there, a bicyclist chimes and clicks by.
